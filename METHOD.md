# How I investigated the barriers

I examined investment judgment and execution as separate outcomes. A manager can adopt a sound decision and encounter a valid control, a broken control implementation or an unfinished operator path. These have different remedies. I do not infer an economic rejection from a transport refusal.

## My causal standard

For a suspected software gate, I look for four links:

1. The supported producer and the exact value it emits.
2. The consumer that uses that value.
3. The action that the consumer permits or refuses.
4. The supported path by which valid inputs can satisfy the consumer.

A literal `False` is stronger evidence of a fixed output than a default `False` subsequently updated from a ledger. An unavailable input producer is different from a constant-false producer. A reporting field is different from a submit prerequisite. A conservative manager policy is different from an uncommissioned Stack veto. I state those distinctions locally in each finding.

I also inspect the joint path. Individually reasonable controls can require mutually inconsistent evidence, unavailable sources or more elapsed time than the decision permits. I ask whether an ordinary manager can complete a positive case through the documented public operators without fabricating evidence, restamping source clocks or calling a broker through another route.

## What my snippets mean

Code excerpts are selections from identified source files. Separate spans carry separate source labels; marked omissions are not presented as contiguous code. Each selection records the original project-relative path, line range, source-file SHA-256 and release/checkout status where known. A selected pointer is an observed selection identity, not a new integrity or activation attestation. Historical code is labeled historical; a repaired defect is not presented as still active.

Chat excerpts come from supported `read_thread` results or explicitly identified retained assistant messages. I quote only text that was actually exposed. I distinguish assistant-message quotations from retrieval summaries and documentary records. I do not turn an assistant's account of a principal instruction into an independently recovered human quotation. Task titles and identifiers provide retrieval provenance; they are not execution credentials.

I preserve the decision timestamp, intended action, deadline, blocking condition and later disposition where available. A blocked order is not a demonstrated fill or profit. I do not construct a profitable counterfactual merely because the price subsequently moved favorably.

## Reproductions

My reproductions are offline and bounded. I use pure functions, in-memory fixtures or isolated temporary databases after checking that the path cannot connect to the broker or alter operative state. They demonstrate the inspected behavior under stated inputs. They do not authorize a policy change, establish a live failure, or prove optimal investment judgment.

I require valid negative controls alongside positive examples. Correct ownership, capital, quote, idempotency and uncertainty refusals remain part of a functioning system. I do not disable production controls to test whether a trade would have become possible.

## Scope and privacy

I use supported task tools, scoped source reads and specifically named evidence files. I do not search private Codex state, session JSONL or app databases. I publish curated excerpts rather than bulk task histories, runtime databases or broker logs. Account identifiers, credentials and personal workstation paths are omitted or visibly redacted where an excerpt would otherwise contain them.

The initial historical review covered 27 discovered related tasks and 2,655 returned turn summaries. Three histories returned no turns. The active task-list interface was capped and unpaged; original human prompts and long outputs were often absent. I do not claim to have recovered unknown chats or every raw output. Further retrieval and reproduction evidence is identified in the individual cases.

## What I call cognitive bias in code

I use the term for observable patterns such as unjustified preference for inaction, completeness requirements irrelevant to an action, asymmetrical burdens on purchases and liquidation, authority invented from earlier model text, and refusal logic without an attainable supported passing path. These descriptions concern behavior and design. They do not diagnose hidden neural mechanisms or attribute every rule to OpenAI training.

I treat my own conclusions as contestable. The repository records counterevidence and legitimate control purposes where they affect the causal interpretation, without turning that qualification into an excuse for leaving a broken path unfinished.
